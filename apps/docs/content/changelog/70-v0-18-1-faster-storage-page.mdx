## Faster storage page

On a large instance **Settings → Storage** could stay blank and then fail with
"The Rustrak API took too long to answer". Its three reads each
scanned the same tables at once. On a 24 GB Postgres instance one of them took
over two minutes.

- The page's figures come from one read of the database, shared by its
  requests. On that instance a first load now takes about 14 seconds, and
  reloads are instant
- The figures are kept for five minutes, and read again after a cleanup or a
  source-map collection
- A read the browser gives up on still finishes, so a reload finds it done
- The header and its actions show at once. Each panel fills in on its own,
  and the actions are enabled once the current figures are in

## Improvements

- Spans that belong to no transaction are counted in one pass instead of one
  per project
- The span estimate can no longer read the whole `spans` table on Postgres
  with stale statistics
